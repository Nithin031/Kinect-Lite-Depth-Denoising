    What the demo screen shows

    1. Raw camera feed – what a normal camera sees
    2. Raw depth – the sensor's view, with its noise
    3. Median-cleaned depth – the cleanest-looking depth
    4. Camera movement – the path ICP works out, against the true path
    5. ICP error – how far each path is from the truth, over time
    6. ICP maps – the room rebuilt from those positions
