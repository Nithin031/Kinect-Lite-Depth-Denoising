Variants:
  point: point-to-point (SVD of the matched pairs)
  plane: point-to-plane (distance along the target normal)
  plane-tukey: point-to-plane with a Tukey robust loss (k = half the matching distance)
  gicp: generalized ICP (plane-to-plane, covariance per point)
